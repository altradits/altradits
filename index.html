Questions owners ask

Is the work done by AI or by a person?

Both, deliberately. Each department has an AI agent that does the work fast, using that department's written playbook. A quality reviewer checks every deliverable before it reaches you. Stanley takes the real-world steps (like posting from your account or making a booking you've approved), handles anything sensitive, and personally follows up if you rate any job below 4 stars.

Why did the chat ask me to pay 100 sats?

To keep Altra fast and available for real clients, chats that look automated, abusive or unrelated to our services are paused until a 100-sat Lightning payment (a few US cents) confirms a real person. Pay from any Lightning wallet and the chat reopens automatically, or simply email altradits@gmail.com instead.

How do you keep my accounts and data safe?

Access through delegated logins or a password manager share, never passwords in plain email. Least access needed, removed when we finish. Your data is never used to train AI models, and a simple confidentiality agreement is available on request.

I'm in a different time zone. Does that work?

Yes. Clients are worldwide. Europe, Africa and the Middle East get same-day overlap; the Americas get early-evening overlap; Asia-Pacific wakes up to finished work. Altra answers instantly at any hour.

What if I don't like the result?

Start with the free first task, no commitment. On paid work, if what's delivered doesn't match the proposal it's fixed free for 14 days. Retainers are month to month.

How do I pay?

Card, PayPal, Wise, M-Pesa or Bitcoin over Lightning. Retainers are paid monthly in advance; projects take a 50% deposit and the rest on delivery.

What don't you do?

Anything that needs a licensed professional (legal, medical, regulated financial advice), anything unlawful, and holding anyone's crypto keys. Altra will say so plainly and point you somewhere better.
