Quick answers

Does it cost anything?

Opening a wallet is free, and payments between Ye₿ente wallets are free. Payments out over Lightning pay the network's small routing fee; M-Pesa payouts add the payout partner's 1% fee.

What if someone takes my phone?

They still can't send: every payment needs your PIN or fingerprint, and five wrong PINs lock payments. If your email is on the wallet, log in from another phone and remove the lost one.

What if I lose my phone?

Log in on a new phone with your email (if you added one) and PIN. Forgot the PIN, or no email? Your 12 words or your email bring the wallet back with a new PIN; for 24 hours after that, at most 1,000 sats can leave, in case it isn't you.

Who holds my money?

To start, we do. Ye₿ente is a starter wallet: Altradits holds small amounts for you on its own Lightning node, so your wallet opens in a minute and is easy to recover. That is why balances are capped for everyday amounts. When you're ready, we help you move to a wallet only you control, free.
