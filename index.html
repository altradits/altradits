Before you pay

What exactly do I get?

Exactly the ticked list on the card you paid for, delivered by the time shown. Nothing is open-ended: if a card says 12 posts, you get 12 posts.

How do I pay, and are there fees?

Click any price and a QR appears for that exact amount. Scan it with any Bitcoin wallet: Lightning pays in seconds, any other wallet or exchange pays on-chain. Altradits adds no fee; your wallet's small network fee is shown before you pay.

How do you know what I need?

The payment window has a box for your email and what you need, before or after paying. You also get a client portal to add files, request changes and approve the work.

Why are quick jobs so affordable?

Each is small and fixed, and AI agents do the first pass in minutes with a person checking it. You pay for a result, not hours.

My task isn't listed. Can I still order?

Yes: buy the 2-hour pay-as-you-go block and describe the task. For ongoing work, a monthly plan covers any mix of the services listed here.

Is the work done by AI or by a person?

Both, deliberately. An AI agent works fast from a written playbook, a quality reviewer checks every deliverable, and our freelancer team, led by Stanley, takes the real-world steps and handles anything sensitive.

Can you do it urgently, within minutes or hours?

Yes: 100+ freelancers are on standby. Choose "Within a few hours" or "Right now" when you order. Once you've paid, you'll see Stanley's direct number to flag it, and your job jumps the queue.

How do you keep my accounts and data safe?

Access through delegated logins or a password manager share, never passwords in email. Least access needed, removed when we finish. Your data is never used to train AI models.

What if the result doesn't match the card?

It's fixed free for 14 days after delivery. Monthly plans can be cancelled any month from your portal.

What don't you do?

Anything that needs a licensed professional (legal, medical, regulated financial advice), anything unlawful, and holding anyone's crypto keys.
