Use your wallet

Three things to know. Thirty seconds.

  • Sats

    You count in sats

    Bitcoin is too big to spend daily, and dollars need banks. So you count in sats: 100 million per bitcoin.

  • In

    Get some in

    Share your address or QR. Sats arrive in seconds. Start with a tiny amount.

  • Out

    Send some out

    Paste an address or scan a QR, enter sats, approve with your PIN.
