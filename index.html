For the shop: three steps

  1. 1

    Register your M-Pesa number. Add your shop name and a 6-digit PIN. It takes a few minutes.

  2. 2

    Print your QR. One QR on the counter. Print it, or save it as a picture.

  3. 3

    Get paid in shillings. Each payment lands in M-Pesa with its code. You see every payment in your history.

For the customer

Scan the QR with any Lightning wallet. Check the shillings on screen. Pay in sats.

Costs

kTown is free. Tando converts the sats to M-Pesa for a 1% fee, paid by the payer on top of the shop's shillings.

What kTown never does

  • It never holds money. It is not a wallet.
  • Payouts go straight to the shop's M-Pesa. Sats go to Tando. Tando pays M-Pesa to the shop's number.

Questions

What is kTown?

kTown is a free QR till for shops. The shop shows one QR at the till. Customers pay in sats over Lightning. The shop gets shillings in M-Pesa.

What does the shop need to start?

A Safaricom M-Pesa number, a shop name, and a phone to register. Then print the QR and show it at the till. Nothing to buy and nothing to install.

What does the customer need?

Any Lightning wallet. They scan the QR, check the shillings on screen, and pay in sats.

Is kTown free?

kTown is free. Tando converts the sats to M-Pesa for a 1% fee, paid by the payer on top of the shop's shillings.

Who holds the money?

Nobody in between. kTown never holds money and is not a wallet. Sats go to Tando, and Tando pays M-Pesa straight to the shop's number.

How does the shop get paid?

In M-Pesa, to the registered number. Each payment lands with its M-Pesa code, and the shop sees every payment in its history.

What if a payment fails?

Payments, refunds and failed payments are between the payer, Tando and M-Pesa. kTown cannot refund or reverse a payment.

Who can join the pilot?

Shops in Kisumu, for now. Places are limited. Registration takes a few minutes.

What does kTown know about me?

For the shop: its M-Pesa number, its shop and M-Pesa names, and its payment records. Nothing about who pays is asked for or stored.

What if kTown stops?

The pilot is free and may stop. If it stops, the shop's Tando address keeps working, because money never passes through kTown.
