code-made templates (Minimal 2027) so there is always a template to design
from. Work lands in four quarterly PRs (Q1 carries this plan and the new
backgrounds). Quality beats speed: a weak card is worse than a draft.

## Card rules

Every 2027 card, and every card Stanley designs later, follows these.

1. One message, one focal point. The headline is the hero. At most one
   piece of art or accent. If you can remove something and the card still
   works, remove it.
2. Whitespace is the design. Keep at least 55 to 60 percent of the canvas
   empty. Margins at least 96 px on every side (match the logo's x 96).
   Nothing touches the edges unless it deliberately bleeds off.
3. A strict grid. 12 columns with 96 px outer margins. Headlines
   left-aligned on one shared left edge (x 96) across the whole year.
   Baselines on an 8 px rhythm. The same headline position within a month,
   so the feed reads as a series.
4. Type hierarchy with two levels only. Headline 120 to 170 px Bricolage
   Grotesque 800, tight line height (1.0 to 1.05), slight negative
   tracking, max 3 lines, balanced line breaks (no single-word last line).
   Subline 52 to 64 px Figtree 600, line height 1.25, about 55 percent of
   the headline's visual weight. Never more than two fonts or three sizes
   on one card.
5. Colour restraint. Ground plus ink plus ONE accent from the palette per
   card. Orange is reserved for Bitcoin and coins, red for emphasis or
   warnings. Text contrast meets WCAG AA (4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text at
   24 px and up, which covers every subline; checked in code for every
   card). No text on busy texture.
6. Repetition with variation. The month motif repeats; the accent
   position, scale and shape change day to day. A month seen as a 7-column
   grid looks like one family with 28 to 31 distinct members.
7. Social-safe. The key words survive a centre 16:9 crop (1920x1080) and
   a 1:1 square crop. Readable at phone thumbnail size (checked at 400 px
   wide).
8. Honest finishing. No drop shadows, gradients, bevels, glows, clip-art,
   emoji or stock cliches (rockets, lambos, piles of cash). Edges
   anti-aliased, one consistent stroke weight per month.
9. Brand lock. Logo and footer in exactly the same place and size on
   every card. The logo zone stays clear (art-check).

## Layout system (shared by the editor and the renderer)

Canvas 1920x1280. Headline block starts at x 96, y 320 (top, fixed within
a month), max width 1152 (x 96 to 1248), Bricolage Grotesque 800, 120 to
170 px to fit, line height 1.02. Subline starts at x 96, 48 px below the
headline block, max width 1152, Figtree 600, 56 px, line height 1.25, red
on every ground (white on navy only if red ever fails its check). Logo
290 px wide at x 96, y 1085 (navy logo on light grounds, white logo on
navy). Footer "altradits.com" in Figtree, right-aligned ending at x 1874,
baseline y 1238, grey on light grounds, light grey on navy. The Bitcoin
sign comes from the site's own Bricolage latin-ext font, never a system
font, so renders stay reproducible.

## Copy rules

One idea per card. Headline max 8 words, subline max 14 words. Caption
under 240 characters including #Altradits and the month tag, plus an
altradits.com link where it fits. Alt text describes the card and its
words, under 200 characters. Plain words, short sentences, active voice.
Teach, do not hype: no price talk, no "to the moon", no "get rich", no
investment advice, no guaranteed returns. Accurate Bitcoin and Lightning
facts only; when unsure, leave it out. Ye₿ente posts show a real feature
that exists in the wallet today (PIN, handle, QR, free handle sends,
invoices, passkeys, M-Pesa buy where switched on); never promise
unreleased features. No headline or caption repeats across 2026-11,
2026-12 and all of 2027.

## Honest custody

Ye₿ente is a custodial wallet: Altradits holds the bitcoin, there are no
recovery words, and it has freeze and pause features. Never say or imply
that Ye₿ente users hold their own keys, that their Ye₿ente money "can't
be frozen", or that they "hold their own money" in Ye₿ente. Self-custody
lessons (keys, 12 words, "their keys, their coins") are fine as general
education in Lesson, Tip, Myth vs fact, Story or Question posts, framed
as a later step ("when you're ready to hold your own keys..."), and must
never sit in a Ye₿ente-type post or suggest Ye₿ente is self-custody. The
marketing suite fails a Ye₿ente-type post that contains: freeze, froze,
frozen, own keys, own money, hold your own, holding your own, nobody
can, self-custody, 12 words, twelve words, seed, ownership, owning.
Confirmed 5 Nov 2026: this rule stands for every quarter.

## Background library (Minimal 2027)

96 backgrounds in the end: 12 monthly motifs times 4 families times 2
variants (A: the main arrangement, B: a second arrangement of the same
motif). Variant A ships as bg-72 to bg-119 in Q1; variant B ships per
quarter (Q1 motifs as bg-120 to bg-131, Q2 motifs as bg-132 to bg-143,
then 12 per quarter). Each has a
matching tpl-NN thumbnail (logo composited, as before). Made from code
with deploy/mkt-bg.py (Python, Pillow and numpy, 2x supersample then
downscale, JPEG quality 85, each file under 150 KB, deterministic: every
shape is an explicit spec, no randomness). Two variants per motif give
each month 8 motif backgrounds, so the usage limits below always fit.
Families:

- Paper: white or cream ground with one accent (rule, dot grid corner,
  ring, coin, arc).
- Ink: navy ground with one light accent (white words and logo).
- Split: two flat colour fields with a hard or curved edge; words sit on
  the left field.
- Frame: a thin motif border, corner brackets or margin marks.

Every background leaves the text zone (x 96 to 1248) and the logo zone
(x 60 to 460, y 1050 to 1225) empty, plus the footer corner. Entries get
names by look, a "family" field, and correct ink and light flags. The
panel groups them as "Minimal 2027" by family, under the existing
groups, keeping lazy-loading and the download link.

Card backgrounds: no two consecutive days share a background, and no
background appears more than 6 times in a month.

## Tools

- deploy/mkt-bg.py: draw the Minimal 2027 backgrounds and thumbnails.
- deploy/mkt-render.py: render a month's cards from its month file, so
  Stanley can re-render after editing copy. Render-time checks (text in
  margins, contrast, line count, size, dimensions) fail loudly.
- deploy/mkt-check.py: verify the shipped files (dimensions, sizes,
  unique copy, background limits, caption lengths) and build contact
  sheets under /tmp/mkt2027-review/ (7-column calendar grid, 400 px
  thumbnails, centre 16:9 crops).
- deploy/art-check.py: extended for navy grounds (white-logo clearance),
  same failure rules as before for light grounds.
