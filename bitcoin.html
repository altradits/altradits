The principles we teach by

Straight from the Bitcoin whitepaper and the way the network still works. Read the whitepaper (9 pages).

  • Peer to peerMoney that moves from one person to another without a bank in the middle.
  • Don't trust, verifyRun your own node and check every payment yourself, instead of taking anyone's word for it.
  • Your keys, your BitcoinHold your own keys. If someone else holds them, they hold your money.
  • 21 million, foreverA supply fixed in the rules, which no one can print more of.
  • Open sourceAnyone can read the code, question it and help improve it. So can you.
  • Privacy by habitA new address for every payment, and only the information a payment needs.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Learning sessions and help getting started cost nothing, with or without sponsors. The Bitcoin network charges its own small fees when you send a payment; those are paid to the network, not to us.

Will you ever hold my Bitcoin or ask for my recovery words?

We never ask for your recovery words: anyone who does, even claiming to be us, is trying to steal from you. Our lessons and setups leave your keys with you. The one exception is one you choose: Ye₿ente, our starter wallet, holds small amounts for you so you can begin in a minute, and we help you move to your own keys when you're ready.

Do you give investment advice?

No. We teach how Bitcoin works and how to use it safely. No price talk, no predictions.

I run a business. Can you help me accept Bitcoin?

Yes, as part of stage 2: we set it up with you, show your team how it works, and your keys stay with you.

How do we partner or sponsor?

Email hello@altradits.com with what you have in mind. Sponsors get a plain report of what their support made possible, and credit if they want it.

Does Ye₿ente cost anything?

Opening a wallet is free, and payments between Ye₿ente wallets are free. Payments out over Lightning pay the network's small routing fee; M-Pesa payouts add the payout partner's 1% fee.

Which PIN do I use?

Just one: the 6-digit PIN you choose. It opens the app and approves every payment. Fingerprint or face can stand in for it once you turn it on.

Do I have to log in every time?

No. Your phone stays signed in with its own security key. After a few minutes away the app locks, and your PIN or fingerprint opens it again.

What if someone takes my phone?

They still can't send: every payment needs your PIN or fingerprint, and five wrong PINs lock payments. If your email is on the wallet, log in from another phone and remove the lost one.

What if I lose my phone?

Log in on a new phone with your email (if you added one) and PIN. Forgot the PIN, or no email? Your 12 words or your email bring the wallet back with a new PIN; for 24 hours after that, at most 1,000 sats can leave, in case it isn't you.

How do I log in on a new phone?

Open Ye₿ente, tap Log in, enter your wallet's email. We email you a code (good for 10 minutes); enter it plus your PIN. Nothing gets reset.

Where is my @altradits.com address?

You choose the name when creating your wallet (optional). After that it lives under the gear (Settings) → Your Lightning address, ready to copy and share. Anyone can pay name@altradits.com from any Lightning wallet.
