Quick answers

Does it cost anything?

Opening a wallet is free, and payments between Ye₿ente wallets are free. Payments out over Lightning pay the network's small routing fee; M-Pesa payouts add the payout partner's 1% fee.

How much do I need to start?

Almost nothing. Start with a few cents to learn the ropes, and add more only when it feels easy.

Which PIN do I use?

Just one: the 6-digit PIN you choose. It opens the app and approves every payment. Fingerprint or face can stand in for it once you turn it on.

Do I have to log in every time?

No. Your phone stays signed in with its own security key. After a few minutes away the app locks, and your PIN or fingerprint opens it again.

Where is my @altradits.com address?

You choose the name when creating your wallet (optional). After that it lives under the gear (Settings) → Your Lightning address, ready to copy and share. Anyone can pay name@altradits.com from any Lightning wallet.

What if someone takes my phone?

They still can't send: every payment needs your PIN or fingerprint, and five wrong PINs lock payments. If your confirmed email is on the wallet, log in from another phone and remove the lost one.

What if I lose my phone?

Log in on a new phone with your confirmed email (if you added one) and PIN. Forgot the PIN, or no email? Your 12 words or your confirmed email bring the wallet back with a new PIN; for 24 hours after that, at most 1,000 sats can leave, in case it isn't you.

How do I log in on a new phone?

Open Ye₿ente, tap Log in, enter your wallet's confirmed email. We email you a code (good for 10 minutes); enter it plus your PIN. Nothing gets reset.

What if I send to the wrong address?

The app shows the address and amount, then asks for your PIN before anything moves. Check those two, and start small: sent payments can't come back.

Who holds my money?

To start, we do. Ye₿ente is a starter wallet: Altradits holds small amounts for you on its own Lightning node, so your wallet opens in a minute and is easy to recover. That is why balances are capped for everyday amounts. When you're ready, we help you move to a wallet only you control, free.
