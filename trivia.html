How it plays

  • Free entry

    Joining costs nothing. No buy-in, no deposit, no ticket.

  • Last one standing

    Three lives. Twenty seconds a question. Wrong or silent answers cost a life. Ties share the win.

  • The server keeps time

    Your clock decides nothing. The server opens and closes every round and checks every answer.

  • Real prizes from donors

    The prize pool is donated sats. Winners split it at the end. Stanley approves every payout.
