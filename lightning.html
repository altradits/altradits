Lightning in plain words.

No jargon. Six questions, six answers.

What is a sat?

A sat is the smallest unit of bitcoin. Small payments are counted in sats, not whole bitcoin.

What is a channel?

Two parties lock bitcoin together once, then pay each other instantly off chain. The chain only sees the open and the close.

What is an invoice?

A payment request with an amount and an expiry. Pay it before it expires or request a fresh one.

Why is it instant and low-fee?

Off-chain payments skip mining. No block wait, tiny fees.

Who holds my money?

With your own keys, you do. With a custodial wallet, the operator does. Know which one you use.

What must I never share?

Seed words. No real wallet or support ever asks for them.
