kTown

A free QR till for shops.

Status: Pilot in Kisumu, open.

  • The shop prints one QR and shows it at the till.
  • Any customer with a Lightning wallet scans it and pays in sats.
  • The shop gets exact shillings in M-Pesa. kTown never holds money. Tando converts for 1%.

Ye₿ente

A starter Bitcoin wallet.

Status: Early access, by invitation.

  • Open a wallet in about a minute, nothing to install.
  • Altradits holds your sats on its own node. Balances stay small.
  • Send and receive over Lightning. Cash out to M-Pesa.
