What you can build

Anything that takes Lightning payments, without running a node.

  • Tips in your app

    Your readers tip your writers. The sats land on your address. The API tracks each payment.

  • Sell digital goods

    One invoice per order, in sats or priced in USD and KES. Webhooks tell you what settled.

  • Split a bill

    Many payers, one destination. Poll settlement or wait for the webhook.

  • Learn Lightning free

    The sandbox simulates the whole flow. Test keys, test invoices, test webhooks. Nothing real moves.

How it works

  1. Register your own Lightning address. Prove it with a 1 sat challenge payment from the wallet behind it.
  2. Your app asks the API for an invoice. It fetches one from your address and checks the exact amount and description.
  3. Your customer pays your address straight. The sats never pass through Altradits.
  4. You poll settlement or wait for a webhook. Signed, retried, logged.

Pricing

One charge. No tiers, no seats, no hidden fees.

0.5% of settled volume, prepaid in sats. Top up your fee balance (100 sats minimum). Each settled payment deducts 0.5% rounded up, minimum 1 sat. The statement shows every top-up and fee. Credits are non-refundable, except on account close: unused balance pays out to a verified destination within 7 days.

The sandbox is free. Test keys, test invoices, test webhooks, free test credit. Nothing real moves.

Quickstart

  1. Get a sandbox key. Sign in on the dashboard with your email. Create a test key. It starts with ak_test_ and can never touch mainnet.
  2. Create an invoice. To the simulator address, free.

curl

curl -X POST https://altradits.com/api/dev/v1/invoices \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ak_test_your_key_here" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: order-123" \
  -d '{"destinationId":"DEST_ID","sats":5000,"memo":"order 123"}'

JavaScript

const res = await fetch("https://altradits.com/api/dev/v1/invoices", {
  method: "POST",
  headers: {
    "Authorization": "Bearer ak_test_your_key_here",
    "Content-Type": "application/json",
    "Idempotency-Key": "order-123"
  },
  body: JSON.stringify({ destinationId: "DEST_ID", sats: 5000, memo: "order 123" })
});
const { invoice } = await res.json();
// Show invoice.bolt11 as a QR. Your customer pays your address straight.
  1. Settle it and receive a webhook. In the sandbox, play the customer with one call. Register a webhook URL first, then watch the signed invoice.settled POST arrive.
curl -X POST https://altradits.com/api/dev/v1/sandbox/settle \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ak_test_your_key_here" \

Limits

  • 1 to 100,000,000 sats per invoice.
  • 100 invoices an hour per key. 2,000 invoices and 20 top-ups a day per account.
  • Webhook retries after 1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 12 hours. Then the delivery is marked dead.
  • Past a limit, the API answers 429. Wait and retry.

Security

  • Keys are shown once, stored hashed, and scoped: invoices, webhooks, or both.
  • Secrets never land in logs. Abuse is watched and guessing is locked out.
  • The site key that issues top-up and challenge invoices can only receive. It can never spend.
  • In a security lockdown, new top-ups freeze. Invoices, reads and retries continue.

Support
