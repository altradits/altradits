Who makes your wallet work

  • Btrust

    Backs the learning community

    Btrust sponsors BitDevs Kisumu, where Ye₿ente's builder learns Bitcoin alongside other developers. Better builders, better wallet.

    Visit Btrust

  • Minmo

    The person-to-person model

    Minmo's agents help their own communities buy and sell bitcoin, person to person. That is the model for how Ye₿ente spreads. Their team also mentored Ye₿ente's builder into open source.

    Visit Minmo

  • Tando

    Turns your sats into M-Pesa

    When you send sats to a phone number or till, Tando's rails carry them the last mile. Every M-Pesa number and till, reachable from your wallet.

    Visit Tando

  • weVenture Hub

    Trained your wallet's builder

    This startup hub in Addis Ababa hosted the Lightning Developer Bootcamp where Ye₿ente's builder learned routing and channel management: the skills your payments run on.

    Visit weVenture Hub

  • Wallet of Satoshi

    The simplicity bar, and the backup

    The simplest Lightning wallet in the world. It is also Ye₿ente's fallback when our own node is down, so your payments still go through.

    Visit Wallet of Satoshi

  • LakeHub

    Home of the learning community

    BitDevs Kisumu meets at LakeHub, which also backs it. Ye₿ente keeps improving there, alongside more than 100 developers.

    Visit LakeHub

  • My First Bitcoin

    Free education, worldwide

    Makers of free Bitcoin education used around the world. In conversation about growing it in Kisumu next, with developers from LakeHub.

    Visit My First Bitcoin

Simple, because others showed the way

Ye₿ente is built in Kisumu, learning from teams who made Bitcoin easy to start. Thank you.

  • Australia · since 2019
    Wallet of Satoshi

    A Lightning wallet built to be simple: "Bitcoin. Simple." Your first sats in a minute.

    For your wallet: the bar for simplicity: open and pay in a minute.

  • Nairobi, Kenya
    Bitika

    Buy Bitcoin with shillings over M-Pesa or Airtel, from KES 10, with no account.

    For your wallet: the bar for buying small: start with KES 10, not a bank form.

  • Kenya · since 2024
    Tando

    Turns M-Pesa numbers and tills into Lightning payments, so a Kenyan vendor can be paid in Bitcoin without changing anything.

    For your wallet: powers Send Mobile: every M-Pesa number and till.

  • Kenya · since 2025
    Minmo

    Started as a tool for Bitsacco members. Now agents serve their own community, buying and selling Bitcoin with people they know.

    For your wallet: the model for spreading: person to person, through people you trust.

Built in Kisumu, Kenya's third-largest city, on the shore of Lake Victoria, shaped by teams from Australia to El Salvador to Kenya. The goals, not claims: households start on Ye₿ente, then learn to hold their own keys; traders get paid in sats through the M-Pesa tills they already use; developers learn at BitDevs Kisumu and contribute to open source; everyone learns free through Bitcoin Kisumu.
