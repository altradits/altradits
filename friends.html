From where they build, to Kisumu

Four teams made Bitcoin simple to start. Each began in one place, for the people around them. That's what I want for Kisumu.

  • Australia · since 2019
    Wallet of Satoshi

    A Lightning wallet built to be simple: "Bitcoin. Simple." Your first sats in a minute.

    For Kisumu: anyone with a phone holds their first sats in a minute, through Ye₿ente.

  • Nairobi, Kenya
    Bitika

    Buy Bitcoin with shillings over M-Pesa or Airtel, from KES 10, with no account.

    For Kisumu: a fisherman or a student starts with KES 10, not a bank form.

  • Kenya · since 2024
    Tando

    Turns M-Pesa numbers and tills into Lightning payments, so a Kenyan vendor can be paid in Bitcoin without changing anything.

    For Kisumu: Kibuye market traders take sats on the till they already have.

  • Kenya · since 2025
    Minmo

    Started as a tool for Bitsacco members. Now agents serve their own community, buying and selling Bitcoin with people they know.

    For Kisumu: trusted local agents, so Bitcoin spreads person to person.

Kisumu is Kenya's third-largest city, on the shore of Lake Victoria, and home to LakeHub. The goals, not claims:

  1. Households: start on Ye₿ente, then learn to hold their own keys.
  2. Traders: paid in sats through the M-Pesa tills they already use.
  3. Developers: LakeHub's Go developers learn Bitcoin at BitDevs Kisumu and contribute to open source.
  4. Everyone: free education through Bitcoin Kisumu.

How I work with partners

  1. Start the conversation. With a company our 100+ Go developers at LakeHub would value.
  2. Hand over the lead. When we partner, a group takes ownership.
  3. Everyone benefits. I keep building tools for the community.
  4. We learn together towards full-time open-source work. Follow the tracker.

It hasn't been easy. The Bitcoin community has given us enough hope. I can't wait to see what's next.
