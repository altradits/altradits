How it works

  1. 1Someone posts a task. Every task is checked by a person before it appears.
  2. 2You answer through the contact on the task. Agree the pay before you start.
  3. 3They pay you directly. Sats go to your own wallet. M-Pesa goes to your phone.

Altradits never holds money. Pay happens between the poster and the worker. Small tasks only. Meet in public. Never pay to get a task.

    Stay safe

    • Agree the pay before you start. In sats: share your Lightning address only when the poster confirms the task.
    • Meet in public. Tell someone where you go.
    • Never pay to get a task. A task that asks for a fee is a scam. Flag it.
    • Small tasks only. New to sats? The Library teaches your first wallet.

    Board rules and privacy

    • The Kazi board is a noticeboard. Altradits lists tasks. It never employs, pays or guarantees anyone.
    • Pay is agreed between poster and worker, before work starts. Altradits never sees or holds it.
    • Posters: only small, legal tasks in or around Kisumu. No advances from workers, ever.
    • What is stored: the words on your task, your name and contact line, the time, and a code that lets you edit or close it. The contact line shows on the public board. That is its purpose.
    • Tasks close after 30 days. Ask for deletion any time through Contact.
    • Pilot: free, may stop. Rules version 2026-11-06.
