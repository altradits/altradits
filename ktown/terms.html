Merchant terms (pilot)

  1. kTown never holds money and is not a wallet. It shows your Tando address as a QR code on your counter.
  2. Tando (bitcoin.co.ke) converts the sats into M-Pesa for a 1% fee, paid by the payer on top of your shillings.
  3. Payments, refunds and failed payments are between the payer, Tando and M-Pesa. kTown cannot refund or reverse a payment.
  4. This is a pilot: kTown is free and may stop. If it stops, your Tando address keeps working, because money never passes through kTown.

Privacy notice

  1. kTown stores your M-Pesa number, your shop and M-Pesa names, and your payment records: amount, shillings, payment hash, M-Pesa code and time. Nothing about who pays you is stored, because it is never asked for.
  2. These records run your history and prove what landed in M-Pesa.
  3. To close your account and delete your names and number, message the help contact shown in the app. Payment records are kept for the books.

Masharti ya mfanyabiashara (majaribio)

  1. kTown haishiki pesa wala sio pochi. Inaonyesha anwani yako ya Tando kama msimbo wa QR kwenye kaunta yako.
  2. Tando (bitcoin.co.ke) hubadilisha sats kuwa M-Pesa kwa ada ya asilimia 1, inayolipwa na mlipaji juu ya shilingi zako.
  3. Malipo, marejesho na malipo yaliyoshindikana ni kati ya mlipaji, Tando na M-Pesa. kTown haiwezi kurejesha au kubatilisha malipo.
  4. Huu ni mpango wa majaribio: kTown ni bure na unaweza kusimama. Ukisimama, anwani yako ya Tando inaendelea kufanya kazi, kwa sababu pesa haipitii kTown kamwe.

Taarifa ya faragha

  1. kTown huhifadhi nambari yako ya M-Pesa, majina ya duka lako na ya M-Pesa, na rekodi za malipo yako: kiasi, shilingi, hash ya malipo, kodi ya M-Pesa na muda. Hakuna taarifa kuhusu anayekulipa inayohifadhiwa, kwa sababu haiulizwi kamwe.
  2. Rekodi hizi zinaendesha historia yako na kuthibitisha kilichofika M-Pesa.
  3. Kufunga akaunti yako na kufuta majina na nambari yako, mtumie ujumbe anwani ya msaada iliyo kwenye programu. Rekodi za malipo huhifadhiwa kwa vitabu.
